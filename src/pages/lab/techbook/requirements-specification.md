### SCN-001 — Request, assignment and confirmation

The requesting user submits a web request with site address, issue description, Service or Breakdown type, preferred times, account contact details and, optionally, a different on-site contact. Alternatively, an admin selects a registered client company and requesting contact and submits the same required information on their behalf; the creator, represented client and creation time are recorded. Creation does not confirm the visit. Admins receive in-app and email alerts. An admin manually assigns a technician before confirmation; the client, admin or assigned technician can propose a time. The client can confirm the proposed date and time via a link in the proposal email, WhatsApp or the app (DEC-093–095, DEC-097, DEC-099). The email link must work for a client without the app; email reply handling is not required. The link uses a cryptographically secure token bound to the intended client, request and current proposal/version and expires after 24 hours or earlier invalidation. Opening it only displays proposal details. The client must explicitly select **Confirm Date & Time** and complete a one-time verification code delivered to the registered email. The code expires after 10 minutes, is invalidated after five incorrect attempts, and no more than three codes may be issued per client/proposal per hour; issuing a new code invalidates the previous one. Successful confirmation consumes the link and code. Changed proposals, cancelled requests and revoked permissions invalidate the link; the forwarded link alone is insufficient. Before saving, recheck identity, authorisation, current proposal, assignment and booking status. Expired, stale, superseded, revoked or used credentials cannot cause a new state change; failed verification does not change booking state, and duplicate submissions do not duplicate confirmation or notifications. Technician-or-admin acceptance can be provided via the app or WhatsApp. Client and staff agreement may arrive in either order and must identify the same current proposal; staff acceptance also applies to the current assignment. Client confirmation is the saved agreement, not the final appointment status: an admin sends the conditional final-confirmation email and the appointment becomes Confirmed only after both saved agreements, mail-service acceptance and a successful state save. The app displays authoritative current status. A changed date, time or location invalidates both agreements. Reassignment invalidates staff acceptance, retains client confirmation only when those details are unchanged, and returns a previously Confirmed visit to Pending until fresh staff acceptance and final email acceptance are saved. Preserve history and notify affected parties. On removal, revoke the former technician's assignment-based access immediately unless independently authorised, and send a generic unassignment notice by in-app and email without booking information or access links. Record access revocation and notification outcome (DEC-080, DEC-084, DEC-088–089).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, intended scope; DEC-005, DEC-006, DEC-008, DEC-021, DEC-026, DEC-029, DEC-049, DEC-052, DEC-053, DEC-071, DEC-072, DEC-074, DEC-079, DEC-080, DEC-084, DEC-088–089, DEC-093–095, DEC-097, DEC-099.

### SCN-002 — Declined or conflicting time

If a client declines an individual proposed time or a participant identifies a clash, the request remains Pending, not Declined, and a new time can be proposed with fresh technician-or-admin acceptance and client confirmation. Only an admin may mark a Pending request Declined with a reason; if no time works, an admin may instead close it as Unresolved with a reason. Record actor and timestamp for either outcome. Clash handling is manual; automated detection and matching are not required (DEC-085).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, intended scope; DEC-005, DEC-017, DEC-085.

### SCN-003 — Rescheduling

The client requests rescheduling through the web app or WhatsApp before work starts. A saved reschedule request immediately supersedes any existing confirmation, preserves the prior appointment in history and keeps the underlying service request Pending and open; a replacement proposal receives a new identity and requires fresh acceptance, client confirmation and final email. If a new time is not yet available, the request remains Pending until an authorised participant supplies it. For an In Progress visit, record the request and client's reason, notify the admin and assigned technician, and leave the visit In Progress without automatically cancelling, rescheduling or changing its status. The admin or assigned technician may continue work or close the visit as Unresolved with a mandatory reason, actor and timestamp. If a further visit is necessary, create a new booking request linked to the original; it follows normal acceptance and confirmation. Completed, Cancelled, Declined and Unresolved visits cannot be rescheduled (DEC-077, DEC-078).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, intended scope; DEC-010, DEC-011, DEC-026, DEC-038, DEC-040, DEC-052, DEC-076, DEC-077.

### SCN-004 — Breakdown request

The client selects Breakdown. The admin acknowledges the request, tries to schedule a time, phones the on-site contact or, if none is provided, the requester, and logs a response by 17:00 on the effective receipt day. A logged unanswered call counts as a response. For Service, the admin acknowledges or updates the request by 17:00 on the business day after effective receipt. Business hours and effective receipt follow DEC-068. An unconfirmed proposal, including one less than four business hours away, remains Pending; the four-hour threshold has no separate state change or required notification (DEC-060, DEC-078).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, success measures; DEC-018, DEC-023, DEC-042–045, DEC-048, DEC-053, DEC-054, DEC-056, DEC-057, DEC-059, DEC-060.

### SCN-005 — Email failure and follow-up

A definitively failed email, including a final-confirmation email, is retried up to three times after the initial attempt within 60 minutes, subject to current booking status. For the selected Resend REST API, record the send attempt and available provider identifiers and use authenticated webhook events for delivery, bounce and failure tracking (DEC-081). If final-email acceptance is uncertain, automatic confirmation processing and retries of that uncertain send are paused until an admin reconciles it; refer the issue to the admin. A permitted cancellation may be saved during transmission or uncertainty and takes precedence over unfinished confirmation. Delayed responses cannot restore a cancelled visit, confirm an obsolete assignment or confirm a superseded proposal. If the email was already sent before cancellation, issue the applicable cancellation notification and retain the history. After retries fail, flag "Notification failed" for admin follow-up. A visit remains Confirmed if its accepted final email later fails delivery, unless a subsequent permitted action changes it. A failed in-app alert is recorded and visible to admins; the request remains in the admin work queue and email is used as an alternative where available (DEC-067, DEC-079, DEC-084, DEC-091).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, unacceptable outcomes; DEC-022, DEC-024, DEC-026, DEC-027, DEC-029, DEC-067, DEC-079–081.

### SCN-006 — Access and completion

The assigned technician and admins can see a request. Company users access their own bookings by default; admins may grant or revoke additional same-company view/cancel permission (DEC-069). The technician uses a mobile-friendly web schedule to see allocated appointments and can accept in the web app or via WhatsApp. Only the currently assigned technician can start a Confirmed appointment, including early, recording the actual start timestamp and changing the status to In Progress. The technician records completion with notes in the web app. Notes are internal by default; only explicitly client-visible completion notes appear to authorised clients, and internal admin notes never do. Record visibility changes. If work cannot continue, an admin or the assigned technician may instead close it as Unresolved with a reason, actor and timestamp (DEC-076, DEC-084, DEC-087).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, privacy and security; DEC-014, DEC-019, DEC-051, DEC-055, DEC-069, DEC-072, DEC-074, DEC-076, DEC-084, DEC-087.

### SCN-007 — Cancellation and outcomes

Before work starts, the requester, authorised same-company user, assigned technician or admin can cancel; the saved cancellation, record and notifications follow DEC-065. No actor can directly cancel an In Progress or Completed visit. A client request to stop an In Progress visit is referred to the admin and assigned technician without automatically cancelling or closing it; if work cannot continue, either authorised staff member may close it as Unresolved with a reason, actor and timestamp (DEC-076). Clients can view permitted current and historical requests and relevant status changes. At the LAB cutoff, Confirmed, In Progress and Completed each count once for scheduling success; In Progress does not mean completed. These and Cancelled, Declined or Unresolved qualify for completeness, with reasons for the latter two; Pending fails (DEC-075).

**Sources:** Business Intent Brief, success measures; DEC-013, DEC-030, DEC-031, DEC-065, DEC-066, DEC-070, DEC-074, DEC-075, DEC-076.

### SCN-008 — WhatsApp booking

**Gate 2 approved under DEC-100.** Initial scope includes WhatsApp conversations and creating, confirming, rescheduling and cancelling bookings. AI may create or cancel a booking based on a WhatsApp conversation only after the client explicitly confirms that action. AI may record the client's visit-time confirmation and reschedule requests. In Progress reschedule requests follow SCN-003: record the request and reason, notify the admin and assigned technician, and do not automatically change the visit; terminal visits cannot be rescheduled (DEC-077). For first-version client access, the sender's number must be on the authorised list managed by the admin; each number maps to one user linked to one company (DEC-063, DEC-069). Admins also maintain an authorised staff list linking technician and admin numbers to their staff accounts and roles (DEC-073). Ambiguous messages and unlisted numbers are referred to an admin. An authorised company user may cancel a colleague's booking through WhatsApp, subject to explicit client confirmation and DEC-076's status restrictions. A client request to stop an In Progress visit is referred to the admin and assigned technician, without automatic cancellation or closure. An assigned technician or authorised admin may accept the proposed time via WhatsApp (DEC-069, DEC-072). Technician or admin acceptance, client confirmation and admin final email are required (DEC-052). Technical sender authenticity and platform consent require evidence (FND-005); failure handling follows DEC-067.

### SCN-009 — Operational oversight and event notifications

An admin uses a dashboard to review open requests, assignments, pending confirmations, deadlines, notification failures and daily or weekly technician schedules, with Breakdown prioritised over Service and proposed times distinguishable from Confirmed visits. An unanswered deadline is Approaching with at most two business hours remaining, and Overdue after it passes; the approved business calendar applies. Request, assignment, proposal, confirmation, reschedule, cancellation and completion events generate the notifications in REQ-025. A saved action is not reversed by notification failure; failures are visible for follow-up. `/health` returns HTTP 200 only when application and database health checks succeed, and HTTP 503 when the application responds but a health check fails. The external monitor treats non-200 responses, network failures and requests exceeding five seconds as failures; it runs nominally every five minutes, alerts the designated LAB administrator after three consecutive scheduled failures and records recovery (DEC-099). Configure the recipient during deployment. Use a free service; verify the UptimeRobot Free candidate in Design and select a no-cost alternative if needed without changing the approved behavior. No paid monitoring or production SLA is required. Provider and operational feasibility remain unverified (FND-006).

## Lifecycle and confirmation rules

These approved baseline rules refine DEC-065–091 and are subject to Gate 2 approval DEC-100 and Gate 3 baseline approval DEC-101.

- A **service request** is the client's need and is the unit of LAB measurement. An **appointment** is a proposed or agreed visit for that request; a request can have historical superseded appointments and at most one current appointment. A further visit under DEC-077 is a distinct, linked service request and is counted separately.
- The request's displayed status is Pending when there is no confirmed current appointment, Confirmed or In Progress while the current appointment has that state, and Completed, Cancelled, Declined or Unresolved when that final request outcome is saved. Appointment proposals are Pending until confirmed; superseded is an appointment-history disposition, not a Cancelled request outcome. Preserve identities, time, actor, old/new states, proposal and agreement history; do not overwrite or delete earlier appointments when rescheduling.
- A request can be created Pending by the client, authorised admin on the client's behalf, or AI after explicit client confirmation. An admin assigns a technician manually before confirmation; an authorised client, admin or assigned technician may propose a visit time. Reassignment preserves prior appointment and agreement history and invalidates staff acceptance; retain client confirmation only if date, time and location are unchanged (DEC-084).
- For client confirmation through a proposal-email link, bind a cryptographically secure token to the intended client, request and proposal/version and expire it after 24 hours or earlier invalidation. The registered-email verification code expires after 10 minutes and is invalidated after five incorrect attempts; issue no more than three codes per client/proposal per hour and invalidate the prior code when a new one is issued. A successful confirmation consumes both. Opening the link is display-only; the client must explicitly confirm and verify the code. Changed proposals, cancelled requests or revoked permissions invalidate the link. Recheck identity, authority, assignment, current proposal and status before saving; failed verification or stale/revoked/used credentials do not change state (DEC-097, DEC-099).

| From | Action / authorised actor | To and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Client, admin or assigned technician proposes a time; client and staff provide separate agreement | Pending: current proposal and agreements are saved; neither agreement alone confirms. A decline or manually identified clash leaves the request Pending for a fresh proposal. |
| Pending | Admin reassigns technician | Pending with prior staff acceptance invalidated; retain client agreement only for unchanged date, time and location, preserve history and notify affected parties subject to authorisation. |
| Pending | Admin sends final email after current proposal has both agreements and an assigned technician | Confirmed only after mail-service acceptance and successful save for that same still-current proposal and assignment; a prior final email cannot be reused after reassignment. |
| Pending or Confirmed before work | Requester, authorised same-company user, assigned technician or admin cancels | Cancelled after save with DEC-065 reason and notification rules; no cancellation succeeds for an unauthorised actor. |
| Pending or Confirmed before work | Client requests rescheduling | Pending: preserve and supersede the old appointment and confirmation, keep the request open, and create a replacement proposal when a new time is supplied. |
| Confirmed | Admin reassigns technician | Pending until fresh staff acceptance and final email; retain client agreement only for unchanged date, time and location, preserve history and notify affected parties subject to authorisation. |
| Confirmed | Currently assigned technician starts | In Progress after actual start time is saved, including before the scheduled time; no automatic time window. |
| In Progress | Assigned technician completes with notes | Completed after save of completion time and identity; Completed is terminal. |
| In Progress | Admin or assigned technician closes when work cannot continue | Unresolved after save of reason, actor and timestamp; Unresolved is terminal. Client stop/reschedule requests only refer to both staff, with status unchanged. |
| Pending | Admin marks Declined, or Unresolved when no proposed time works | Terminal outcome after save of reason, actor and timestamp; an individual proposal decline leaves the request Pending. |

Cancelled, Declined and Unresolved are final request outcomes; Completed is terminal. No terminal state can be rescheduled, restarted, completed again or cancelled; In Progress cannot be directly cancelled. Apart from explicitly permitted transitions, state changes are rejected without changing history or reporting success. Removal revokes assignment-based access immediately upon saving, while independently authorised access is evaluated separately under DEC-069 and DEC-089.

### Proposal identity, concurrency and external email

- Every proposal has an identity or version unique within its request. Save staff acceptance and client confirmation against the same current proposal; they may arrive in either order. Changing date, time or location creates a new proposal and invalidates both agreements. Reassignment invalidates staff acceptance even if an admin accepted; retain the client confirmation only for unchanged date, time and location. Bind staff acceptance and each final email attempt to the current assignment as well as proposal, so a delayed result for a prior assignment cannot confirm the new one. Reject stale or superseded agreements and attempts (DEC-084).
- Verify actor, company, status, current proposal and permissions server-side at the time of each saved action, including AI actions and permissions revoked since an earlier message. Client request creation is not appointment-time confirmation. AI creates or cancels only after explicit client action confirmation and cannot override human final-email authority.
- A repeated message or API call with the same operation identity must not repeat a state change or create another request, cancellation or notification-triggering event. Report an already-recorded result only if its saved outcome can be verified; reject conflicting reuse, stale proposals and unauthorised actions without exposing another company's data.
- Competing changes must be committed as an indivisible conditional state transition: recheck current proposal, assignment, status and permissions at commit; only a valid winning action is saved and losers receive an explicit conflict or rejection. Cancellation saved before confirmation wins over late acceptance; confirmation saved first may subsequently be cancelled before work starts. A late email acceptance must not resurrect a cancelled or superseded appointment or confirm an obsolete assignment.
- **Final-confirmation email terminology (DEC-080):** The final-confirmation email is the admin-initiated message sent after client and staff have agreed to the current proposal, before the database confirms the visit. It communicates the appointment conditionally, not as already confirmed, and directs the client to the app for authoritative current status. Mail-service acceptance is a prerequisite, not itself proof of a saved Confirmed state or delivery.
- Mail-service acceptance is external to the database and cannot be rolled back merely by a database failure. Never report a saved confirmation until both acceptance and the state save succeed. If acceptance is uncertain, pause automatic confirmation processing, refer to an admin and do not automatically retry the uncertain send until reconciled. Record the uncertainty and referral where possible; do not claim a handoff was saved if persistence fails. A permitted saved cancellation wins over an unfinished attempt. Do not let delayed acceptance or retry results restore Confirmed or override a newer proposal; process idempotently and retain the event history. If the email was already sent, issue the applicable cancellation notification (DEC-065, DEC-079). Provider reconciliation and delivery evidence remains unverified (FND-007).

### AC-021 — Lifecycle and LAB measurement (REQ-016; SCN-007; DEC-012, DEC-013, DEC-019, DEC-040, DEC-066, DEC-070, DEC-075)

- **Required behaviour:** DEC-074 adds In Progress to the normal execution lifecycle: Pending → Confirmed → In Progress → Completed. Confirmed is successful scheduling but not terminal; In Progress is not service completion. Completed is terminal and requires the technician to record service completion in the web app with notes. Each request has a current status and traceable history. Cancelled, Declined and Unresolved are final outcomes; Pending identifies the responsible party. A final Declined or Unresolved outcome requires a reason; a proposed-time decline during continued negotiation does not.
- **Proposed measurement:** Record a fixed timestamp at the end of the test run after intended actions and applicable notification retries. Count each currently Confirmed, In Progress or Completed request once for scheduling success. Retain earlier confirmation in history after cancellation or pre-start rescheduling, but do not count it as currently confirmed at cutoff; count a pre-start rescheduled booking only after the replacement time is reconfirmed. An In Progress reschedule request does not change the original status, which counts according to its status at cutoff (DEC-075, DEC-077); any linked further request is counted separately.
- **Acceptance tests:** At a fixed cutoff, reconcile every logged request to its current status and history, including Confirmed, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, Declined, Unresolved and Pending. Verify each Confirmed, In Progress or Completed request is counted once for scheduling, not once per historical event; a later cancellation, Unresolved outcome or unreconfirmed pre-start reschedule is not counted, but a reconfirmed replacement is. Verify an original visit still In Progress after a reschedule request counts once, and any linked further request is separately counted according to its own cutoff status. Verify In Progress is not reported as service completion, required final reasons and a reason-free intermediate time decline. Verify technician web completion and that Completed is terminal.
- **Outcome completeness (DEC-070, DEC-075):** At least 20 logged requests are in the denominator; Confirmed, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, Declined with reason, and Unresolved with reason qualify. A Pending request remains visible, traceable and assigned but fails completeness. The measure passes only at 100%, with no deletion or exclusion to improve it.
- **Boundary tests:** A dataset of 20 requests with 5 Confirmed, 3 In Progress, 2 Completed, 4 Cancelled, 3 Declined with reasons and 3 Unresolved with reasons yields 20/20 (100%) completeness and 10 scheduling successes; report these separately without a scheduling pass threshold. Replace one qualifying request with Pending: 19/20 (95%) fails. Declined or Unresolved without a reason does not qualify. A request with Confirmed → In Progress → Completed history counts once according to its status at cutoff.

### AC-022 / AC-023 / AC-024 — Persistence, alerts and AI failure (REQ-002, REQ-010, REQ-011, REQ-014, REQ-019; SCN-005, SCN-008; DEC-022, DEC-024, DEC-027, DEC-029, DEC-037, DEC-038, DEC-067)

- **Proposed governing principle:** No action is reported successfully completed until its required state change is successfully saved. No booking confirmation or notification claims success before the corresponding business action is saved.
- **Proposed save failure:** Do not report a request as created if it cannot be saved; show a clear error, allow retry, prevent duplicates when an earlier save succeeded but its response was lost, and record technical failure where possible.
- **Proposed in-app alert failure:** Preserve and show the request in the authoritative admin work queue; record and expose the failed alert to admins, and use existing email as an alternative where available.
- **Proposed email and WhatsApp failure:** Apply up to three retries after the initial attempt, within 60 minutes, to definitive failures while the action remains valid. If final-email acceptance is uncertain, pause automatic confirmation and do not automatically retry that send until reconciled by an admin (DEC-079). After retries fail, record and flag the failure for admin follow-up, preserve the booking status and prevent duplicate bookings or cancellations from repeated messages. An accepted final-confirmation email keeps the previously agreed meaning even if later delivery fails.
- **Proposed AI uncertainty or failure:** Do not invent information or permission, or make an unconfirmed change. Refer to an admin; preserve the conversation and relevant request context where technically possible; inform the user that the action was not completed where the channel remains available. Do not silently create, cancel or confirm a booking. If persistence prevents recording a handoff, do not claim it was created.
- **Acceptance tests:** Force a save failure and a lost response after a successful save; verify error/retry and no duplicate or false success. Force an in-app alert failure and verify request visibility, failure record and available email fallback. Exhaust email and WhatsApp retries and verify a recorded admin flag, unchanged booking status and no duplicate actions. Verify a later delivery failure does not reverse Confirmed. Simulate uncertain AI and failed handoff persistence and verify no unconfirmed change or false handoff claim, with user and admin messaging subject to channel and persistence availability.

### AC-025 — Effective receipt and deadlines (REQ-008, REQ-009; SCN-004; DEC-020, DEC-023, DEC-048, DEC-054, DEC-056, DEC-068)

- **Required behaviour:** Business hours are 07:00–17:00 SAST Monday–Friday, excluding South African public holidays. Effective receipt is the actual submission time during business hours; before 07:00 on a business day it is 07:00 that day; at or after 17:00, on a weekend or public holiday it is 07:00 on the next business day.
- **Required deadlines:** For Breakdown, an admin acknowledges, attempts scheduling, phones and logs the response by 17:00 on the effective receipt day; a logged unanswered call counts. For Service, acknowledge or update by 17:00 on the business day after effective receipt; no mandatory Breakdown call applies. These are administrative response deadlines, not visit-completion promises. Retain actual submission time, effective receipt, deadline and actual response time; record late responses as breaches.
- **Acceptance tests:** Check 06:59, 07:00, 16:59 and 17:00 on a business day, plus weekends and consecutive public holidays; verify effective receipt and correct deadlines. Verify a logged unanswered Breakdown call counts, a Service response is due the following business day without a mandatory call, all four timestamps are retained and a late response is recorded as a breach rather than given a changed deadline.

### AC-027 — Company authorisation (REQ-012, REQ-015; SCN-006, SCN-008; DEC-036, DEC-038, DEC-055, DEC-058, DEC-063, DEC-069, DEC-073)

- **Required behaviour:** Each company user belongs to one company in the initial version and can access their own bookings. Additional same-company view/cancel access is disabled by default and may be explicitly granted or revoked only by an admin. WhatsApp uses the same permissions as the web; each authorised client number maps to one registered user and company, with explicit confirmation before AI creates or cancels. Admins maintain the authorised staff number-to-account-and-role mappings.

Reject cross-company operations via changed identifiers, modified requests or WhatsApp instructions without exposing booking details. Technicians have assignment-based access to allocated requests, or access through another independently authorised basis; admins have their authorised administrative access. Removal revokes assignment-based access immediately, as tested under AC-040.
- **Acceptance tests:** Verify default own-booking access, denied same-company access until an admin grants permission, and denial after revocation on both web and WhatsApp. Verify non-admins cannot grant or revoke access. Try cross-company reads and changes using identifiers, modified requests and WhatsApp instructions; verify rejection without details. Verify allocated versus unallocated technician access and admin access.
